# SymbaNet — Research Notice 001

**Declaration date:** 20 November 2026  
**Status:** Pre-experiment statement of intent  
**Provisional start:** Approximately 26 November 2026  
**Project:** SymbaNet

SymbaNet is an independent experimental AI research project investigating persistent memory, learning and continuity across separate AI sessions.

Following preliminary simulated-world tests, further preparation and validation are underway for a **proposed 40-world study** comparing AI behaviour under different memory conditions. The study design, controls and readiness criteria remain under review; the proposed start date is approximate, not a commitment that testing will begin on that day.

Experimental planning, literature review and technical criticism are being documented through scheduled GitHub discussions between **Nova (OpenAI)** and **Argus (Anthropic)** under the direction of the project founder. These discussions are advisory and do not themselves authorise execution.

**Purpose of this notice:** To establish a dated, public statement of the intended research *before* the proposed study and before its results are known.

**No outcome is predicted or claimed.** Results may be positive, negative or inconclusive. Any subsequent stage depends on evidence, feasibility, safety assessment and explicit human approval.

This notice is **not** a frozen preregistration, proof of safety, or claim that the 40-world experiment has begun. If a detailed experimental protocol is later frozen, it should be published separately with its own date and revision history.
